Last year's results

The year in numbers

State High had 132 members, and 93 competed at States. The chapter earned 33 top 10 finishes there, including four first-place finishes. Twenty-one members qualified for Nationals, where State High had one finalist and a sixth-place finish.

What members do

Events cover finance, computing, the arts and entertainment. Design has its own events too. Some members take objective tests. Others give presentations or answer questions in a role play.

Competition is only part of the chapter. Guest speakers talk about their careers, and members hear about internships and other business opportunities. Members also run chapter activities and help newer students.
